      Questions

      What should I wear?

      Comfortable clothes you can move in. Assisted stretch sessions are fully clothed, and you'll never need to get on the floor unless you want to.

      I've had a fall, an injury or a surgery. Is this safe for me?

      Tell Tavia about it on the intake form and during your free consult. Sessions are adjusted to you, and she may ask for your doctor's okay before you start.

      Where do sessions happen?

      In a private gym, in your home, at your workplace or station, or online by video. In-home and on-site visits cover Avon, Plainfield, Brownsburg, Danville, Speedway and west Indianapolis.

      Do you work with kids and young adults with disabilities?

      Yes. Adaptive stretch therapy is offered in small groups and one-on-one, adapted to each body. Parents and caregivers are welcome in every session.

      Which forms do I need before my first session?

      The Client Intake & Assessment and the Liability Waiver. Private training clients also sign a Client Service Agreement, and clients under 18 need a Minor Waiver and Parent Consent form. See all forms.

      How do organization programs work and get billed?

      After a discovery call and a pilot or first event, your organization chooses a monthly program or recurring events. Senior living pricing is listed above; corporate and first responder programs are quoted by program. Invoices come through Square and can be paid online by card or ACH.

      Is my health information private?

      Yes. Intake forms are sent through a secure, HIPAA-compliant service, and only Tavia reads them. Read the privacy notice for details.
